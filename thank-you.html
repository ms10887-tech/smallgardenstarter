Start With These 3 Checks:

1

Is the potty schedule clear enough?

Many accidents happen because the puppy gets too much time between potty breaks, especially after sleep, meals, play, or excitement.

2

Are old accident spots fully cleaned?

Dogs may return to the same area if odor remains. Normal household cleaners may not remove the scent completely.

3

Does your puppy have too much freedom too soon?

A puppy that can wander around the home freely may have accidents before you notice the signs.
