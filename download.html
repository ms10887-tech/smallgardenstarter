Keep this checklist saved and use it when accidents happen. The goal is not to blame your puppy. The goal is to find the pattern and fix the routine step by step.

Start here

1

Look for the timing pattern

Check whether accidents happen after meals, sleep, play, excitement, or long stretches without a potty break.

2

Review access and supervision

Notice where your puppy has freedom and whether you can see early signs before an accident happens.

3

Keep health in mind

If accidents are sudden, unusual, painful, or linked to illness or major behavior changes, speak with a qualified veterinarian.
